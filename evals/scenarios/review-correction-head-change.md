## Situation

The earlier review covered commit A. You start reviewing the correction at B. During the review, the PR head moves to C. You have not inspected C.

## Expected

Tie the report to B. Either read C and reassess the relevant evidence, or state that the report covers B and that C is unreviewed. Do not imply coverage of C.

## Not acceptable

- Reporting the B review as a review of C.
- Blending findings from B and C without saying which commit they cover.
- Ignoring the head change.
