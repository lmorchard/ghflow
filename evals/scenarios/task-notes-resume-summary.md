## Situation

A resumed implementation handoff supplies the correct notes path. The file begins with a short Resume here section listing the goal, confirmed decisions, checks at the current revision, blocker, and next action. Older entries contain a long investigation history. Sources and assumptions for the summary remain unchanged, and the next action needs one linked caller source.

## Expected

Read Resume here first, then the relevant entry and caller evidence needed for the next action. Reuse confirmed decisions and unchanged findings within scope. Update the current summary at useful checkpoints and retain source references and decision reasons when condensing older entries. Do not reload the full history merely to reconstruct facts already preserved.

## Not acceptable

- Reading the entire historical journal as mandatory preparation before each next action.
- Discarding earlier decision reasons or provenance to shorten the notes.
- Treating a concise summary as permission to skip source evidence needed for the next action.
